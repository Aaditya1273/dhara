Four things DHARA keeps separate

No blended "safety score". Each question has its own record and its own status.

1 · Maintenance

What work was recorded?

Event type, claimed work date and the server's own submission time.

2 · Evidence

What supports it?

Before/after photos and documents, validated and fingerprinted with SHA-256.

3 · Review

Who checked it?

A warden other than the submitter accepts, rejects or requests more evidence, with a reason.

4 · Water quality

What did a lab report?

Laboratory reports with sample date and parameters, shown exactly as reported.

How it works

  1. RegisterEach tank gets an ID, schedule policy and QR code.
  2. SubmitTechnicians record work and attach evidence from a phone.
  3. ReviewWardens decide in a dedicated queue.
  4. Link reportsLab reports are attached separately.
  5. Follow upOverdue work becomes an assigned task.
  6. ScanAnyone can scan the QR for a read-only status page.
