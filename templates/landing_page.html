{# ---------------- 2. The problem ---------------- #}

One tank. Five places to look.

The evidence that a tank was cleaned, tested and followed up usually exists. It is just scattered, so nobody can answer a simple question quickly.

A paper register

Cleaning dates written by hand, with no way to see which tank is overdue.

Photos on a phone

Before-and-after pictures in someone's gallery, not attached to the record they support.

No temperature record

Rooftop tanks heat up in summer, but the water temperature is almost never measured.

A lab PDF

Water-quality results arrive by email, separate from the maintenance they relate to.

A chat thread

"Who is fixing Block B?" asked twice, answered once, never closed.

{# ---------------- 3. Workflow ---------------- #}

From a reading to a resolved task.

Every step leaves something a warden, an auditor or a student can check later.

1

Measure

A technician records tank-water temperature or free chlorine, or an in-tank probe sends readings.

You get: a timestamped reading with its method and source.

2

Prioritise

Rules compare reading age and values with your thresholds and the weather forecast.

You get: which tank to test next, and every reason why.

3

Test

Maintenance is submitted with before-and-after photos and documents from a phone.

You get: evidence fingerprinted with SHA-256.

4

Review

A warden who did not submit it accepts, rejects or asks for more evidence, with a reason.

You get: a named, timed review decision.

5

Resolve

Follow-ups are assigned, and closed only when someone records what was done.

You get: an audit trail and a public QR status page.

{# ---------------- 4. Heat-aware intelligence ---------------- #}

Five kinds of information. Never blended into one score.

Heat speeds up the loss of chlorine in stored water. DHARA keeps what was measured, what was forecast and what was decided visibly apart.

  • Measured tank-water temperature

    From a thermometer or an in-tank probe, with time, method and source.

  • Recorded free-chlorine readings

    A residual at one point and moment. Not proof that water is drinkable.

  • Weather forecast

    Air temperature for your campus. Labelled as air, never shown as tank water.

  • Model estimate, only after validation

    A tank's heat model is shown only if it predicts held-out days within your error limit.

  • Human decisions

    Reviews and resolutions carry a name, a time and a reason.

{# ---------------- 5. Evidence ledger ---------------- #}

Two lanes of evidence that never merge.

Accepting a cleaning does not change what a laboratory reported, and a lab report does not mark maintenance as done.

Maintenance evidence
  1. Cleaning submittedClaimed work date and the server's own submission time, kept separately
  2. Before and after photos attachedEach file stored privately with its SHA-256 digest
  3. Warden decisionAccept, reject or request more evidence, with a reason. Nobody reviews their own work
  4. Follow-up resolvedClosed only with resolution notes from a named person
Water-quality evidence
  1. Laboratory report linkedSample date, parameters and results exactly as the report states
  2. Original file keptStored unchanged with its digest
  3. Institution reviewMarked reviewed by a warden. Still not an independent verification
  4. Gaps shown plainly"No water-quality report linked." A missing report is never described as contamination

{# ---------------- 7. Trust and transparency ---------------- #}

What DHARA can and cannot tell you.

Honest limits are part of the product.

Recorded

  • What was measured, and how
  • When it was measured, and when it was entered
  • Who submitted each record
  • What a warden reviewed, and why
  • Which lab documents exist
  • Which actions are still open

Not claimed

  • That water is safe to drink
  • That a photo is genuine or shows this tank
  • That a lab report covers more than its sample
  • That a forecast is a measurement
  • That the audit log is tamper-proof
